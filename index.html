Set sail together for cozy chaos

A cute, co-op pirate action adventure. You and a friend play as two animal pirates, a red panda and a raccoon: cozy little 2D sticker characters who sail together across a colorful 3D island world in a hunt for treasure. Cursed skeletons and huge bosses stand between you and your gold. Combine your abilities in team attacks to win the battle and claim the treasure.

Narrative

Two siblings, a table of stickers, and a whole imaginary sea

Penny and Pierce live in an island town near the beach. As Pierce has grown older, their interests have drifted apart, and both of them have ended up feeling a little lonely.

Mom suggests they play together like they used to. Pierce resists at first because it makes him feel like a kid. Penny is hurt to hear it, but glad to have the company. So they spread out their pirate animal stickers, toys and drawings, and begin to play.

Their shared imagination brings the play to life. You step into the kids' imaginary world and set sail on a cozy, chaotic treasure hunt. The story opens and closes with animated cinematics, and short cutscenes with characters and speech bubbles come before each level.

Four Pillars

Two players talking to each other to solve combat challenges together, in a chaotically fun world

Cute Animals

Collectible stickers come to life: thick outlines, simple shapes, expressive faces. Something a kid puts on a notebook and an adult buys as a vinyl sticker.

Co-op Play

Fight side by side, call out plans and pick each other up. Plenty of fights you can't win alone are easy together.

Chaotic Fun

Smack your partner into a sticker ball, fumble a combo and tumble, both go down and wash back to port. The chaos is the fun.

Action Combat

Melee, dash, ranged and charged attacks. Enemies wind up before they strike, so read the tell, dodge and hit back.

Players should feel happy, surprised, and eager to collaborate. They should smile, laugh and connect.

The Crew

True co-stars, not hero and sidekick

Their differences don't get in the way of teamwork. Their differences are the reason the team works.

Red Panda Explorer

PAWS

Kind, brave, curious, observant, quietly fearless

A brave red panda explorer who believes adventure is better when everyone makes it home together. She notices what others miss, comforts frightened creatures and stands her ground when her friends are in danger. She may look softer than Plunder, but she is not weak or passive.

Current playtest weapon: Sword · quick strikes

  • Attack
    Triple Quick SlashA three-hit arc combo. The third slash knocks enemies back a little.
  • Skill A
    Feint SlashLunge through a crowd, marking enemies for 3s and confusing them for 0.8s.
  • Skill B
    Phantom Blade DashBlink between nearby enemies slashing each one, invincible the whole time.

Raccoon Pirate

PLUNDER

Scrappy, funny, impulsive, dramatic, competitive, loyal, treasure-obsessed, secretly tender

A mischievous raccoon pirate who believes every problem can be solved with quick paws, clever tricks and a little dramatic overconfidence. He is the more chaotic half of the duo. His raccoon markings already give him a bandit look, but he's a lovable rascal, not a villain.

Current playtest weapon: Anchor · heavy hitter

  • Attack
    Double SweepTwo wide sweeps. The second one sends enemies flying about 3 meters.
  • Skill A
    SeaquakeSlam the ground to launch enemies and slow them on landing. The shockwave ignores parries.
  • Skill B
    Overhead SlamA charged slam worth 4× a sword hit. Hold a full charge until your partner is ready.

Team Attack

You only win when your moves work together

Most enemies wear armor, and solo hits just bounce off. Time your strikes together, or use your partner as the weapon.

MELEE × PLAYER

Sticker Ball

Player 1 hits Player 2 with a melee attack. Player 2 curls into a sticker ball, rockets into an enemy, then pops back to normal.

MELEE × MELEE · MELEE × RANGED · RANGED × RANGED

Attack Combinations

Mix melee and ranged moves into different team attacks. Each boss island is built to test one of these pairings.

REVIVE

Pick Each Other Up

Hitting zero health doesn't knock you out of the game. Your partner walks over and holds F or controller B for 3 seconds to revive you. If both of you go down, you wash back to port.

Foes

Foes in the current playtest

Enemies come in plain and armored versions, melee and ranged. Every attack has a clear wind-up, and that's your cue to dodge.

BOSS

Rocky Turtle

Heavy armor

Glows with a curse while its armor holds. Swings a pickaxe, leaps into the water with a big splash and sends rock waves along the ground. Break its shell and it will try to armor up again.

BOSS

Hermit Crab

Medium armor

A shoreline bully with giant claws. It raises a claw to charge before it strikes. Dodge, then crack its shell together.

MINION

Frog Warrior

Light armor

Swarms in groups and goes after whoever is still standing. Herd them together with Plunder's Double Sweep and clear them in one go.

How to Play

Two players, one screen

Before you sail

  1. Player 1 picks a character, then keyboard & mouse or claims a controller
  2. Player 2 does the same. A taken character can't be picked twice
  3. When both are ready, hit "Set Sail" to head to port
